The Imaginary Museum is a thought experiment by Anna Starkey, to explore what the edges of a decentralised museum could be. 
This work was funded by an Arts Council England Developing Your Creative Practice Grant in 2021 - 2022. 
The collection for the Imaginary Museum was contributed by Jessica Bradford, now Director of Collections at the Natural History Museum London, composer Matt Rogers, artists Raquel Meseguer Zafe, Caroline Williams and Bryony Ella.
It feels right that an experiment into decentralising a museum is now archived on GitHub as a free to access snapshot of the thinking behind this, rather than paid for on a website hosting platform. 
I hope you enjoy the museum - if you have thoughts and feedback they are welcome via my website imaginaryastronaut.xyz
